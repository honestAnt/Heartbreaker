# Scumbag Field Manual

[中文](./人渣现场手册.md)

Two volumes, read apart. The men's file is for the kind of man in front of you. The women's file is for the kind of woman, including the ones who turn intimacy into a checkout counter. Chinese is the default file. The link above opens this English version.

---

## Open your ears

Listen. This book does not teach you to be a scumbag. It teaches you the drum pattern before their sentence lands clean.

The cadence is borrowed from Nie Xiao, the lecturer whose tic was “if you got it, applaud.” This manual swaps the applause for a star. The star is for you. Not for them.

Do not buy his success course, his fantasy ticket price, or the company story that later showed up in reports as a judgment debtor. You can steal a rhythm. You do not pay tuition.

Being a scumbag is a behavior, not a chromosome. Both sides do ambiguity, hot-and-cold, and the moral high ground. The scenes below are composites from public posts and explainers. They point at no one you know.

## Feeling

How the relationship gets talked smooth, and how you start smoothing it yourself.

### The undefined almost

What it means：Ambiguity is not shyness. Shyness blushes and still steps forward. This parks you outside the counter. He wants the night and the feelings. He does not want the noun. You ask what this is. He says go with the flow. The flow is his. The insomnia is yours.

What it looks like：Public posts keep a three-year specimen. Calls, rides, nights, a whole romance with no cover. Then a holiday arrives and the feed is spotless, as if you were never there. You say the word together. He says naming it is mercenary. The mercenary move is asking for a badge. He wants an endless trial, and the trial does not pay.

### Love-bombing

What it means：You just met, and the future is already furnished. Marriage next year. A number of children. A city you will leave together. He may not know your street. He already named the kid. That is not depth. That is a pie drawn bigger than your actual Tuesday, so you stop checking Tuesday.

What it looks like：A Zhihu column calls this the chain of extreme emotion. Three refusals, and he is still downstairs. The relationship is one day old, and the phone is already inspected. You are half an hour late and he calls the waiting bliss. Bystanders see a film. The film only shoots the first three weeks. In week four the bombing stops, and you start asking whether you failed the script.

### Hot, then nowhere

What it means：Today you are baby. Tomorrow you are read and unanswered. You pull your heart back, and a good-night lands. That is not a rhythm. That is feeding. The feed is not there to nourish you. It is there so you do not leave. Your mood now grows on his reply time. It stopped growing on you.

### The public heater

What it means：He is warm to you and warm, at the same setting, to every woman in the room. You cool down, and he diagnoses a small mind. The diagnosis is his stationery. The fault is the wholesale. Warmth sold by the batch is a sample. Samples do not come with service.

### Silence as punishment

What it means：A cool-down has a return ticket. This does not. Conflict starts, and he vanishes until you are drafting the self-criticism. He does not want the problem solved. He wants you afraid of opening it. The softer you get, the more the silence owns the room.

What it looks like：Explainers draw the line cleanly. Ordinary silence says I need two hours, then comes back and finishes the talk. Punishing silence names no time, answers no point, and waits for your head to drop. In the public stories, people apologize first after a fight. The apology is for raising the thing. The thing is usually what he did.

## Living

Calendars, tables, feeds, and wallets tell the truth oaths won't.

### The invisible relationship

What it means：Real love needs no display. He says it loudly. Loud, because display leaves evidence. You are absent from the feed, the classmate dinner, the family album. He is not being private about a holiday. He is keeping you in the dark. People in the dark are easy to swap.

What it looks like：A frequent post is two or three years in, and his social world has no record of you. You ask. Public affection is childish, he says. The childish rule binds you alone. He can joke with friends and eat with coworkers. You stay an unreleased feature, and you are not allowed to file a bug.

### Parents, next season

What it means：Meeting the parents lives in the next season. This month the project is tight. Next month the family has a thing. New Year is awkward. Try spring. The seasons rotate. The sentence does not. He is not slow to warm. Slow people name a day. He names a road that is always under construction.

What it looks like：In the composite, he can plan children in week three and still fail to reach your parents in year three. The future is verbally full. He enters none of the physical doors. Later you see it: the talk was full, the calendar was empty. A calendar is harder to fake than an oath, so he never lets you hold it.

### Busy, the pardon

What it means：Busy can be real. Busy as a pardon is the tell. He vanishes while busy, and when he is not busy he still does not explain. You get angry, and busy returns: I can’t help the job, I was born unable to comfort anyone. Born unable is a disability card he issued to his own laziness.

### You are too sensitive

What it means：You saw the text, the location, the missing weekend. He changes the subject to your personality. Checking the phone makes you sick. Crying makes you unstable. One question makes you untrusting. The fact is still on the table. He has dimmed the lamp until you doubt your eyes. That is gaslight. The lamp is fine. His hand is over it.

What it looks like：Headlines already contain the flip: after the cheating is found, the lesson is that you are too sensitive, which is why you imagine things. Sensitivity becomes his amnesty. Later you check your own memory with him before you trust it. When memory needs his approval, you have moved out of your own judgment.

### Control dressed as care

What it means：Checking the phone is called worry. Cutting off your friends is called jealousy, which is called love. Choosing your clothes is called taste. Care asks whether you want it. Control answers for you, then wants thanks for the answer. The more you thank him, the smaller your life gets.

### The flip

What it means：He is late. You mention it. The topic becomes your tone. He lied. You check. The topic becomes your checking. The fault sticks to the person who names the fault. That person is usually you, because he never names it. The one who stays silent looks innocent.

What it looks like：The cold-violence notes and the gaslight notes are one chain. You explain. He does not answer the point. He makes you explain why you are explaining. After three rounds the original event is gone. Only your attitude remains. Attitude is easy to scold, so he manufactures attitude.

### When the newness stops

What it means：Chasing a new person pays him. The catch is dopamine. The staying is boredom. When he is bored he does not say breakup. He thins the contact until you leave first. Then he is the one abandoned. The script is clean. The stain is on your initiative.

## The exit

Recognition without leaving is a manual you paid for and never used.

### Forty-eight hours

What it means：A pause is allowed. An unnamed forever is not. Write your own line: if he vanishes past forty-eight hours and names no return, the talk is over and the relationship is over. A hard line means that after it is crossed you do not hold a meeting about the line.

What it looks like：Some writeups put the exit as leave on contact: cold silence past forty-eight hours, three disappearances, no second full injury. Change the number to fit your life. Do not change this: write the number before the romantic brain comes online. After it is online you will find him a time zone, a signal, a childhood. Childhood is not an amnesty clause.

### Read it to a third person

What it means：Alone with the chat log, you edit him into a better draft. Read it to a friend who is not in love with you. The editing falls off. You hear yourself say he actually has it hard. Your friend does not laugh. Then you hear how thin the sentence is.

What it looks like：In the composites, people later admit every line seemed to have context, and the context was all theirs. A third person hears only outcomes. Dates canceled. A name postponed. Fault reversed. Strung together, that is the manual. You do not need more context. You need a listener who refuses to supply the missing words.

### Three ledgers

What it means：Open three ledgers at once: the name, the time, the money. If you only read the sweet lines, you go broke. If two of the three are overdrawn for a long stretch, stop. This is not a punishment for him. It is your account refusing to be withdrawal-only.

What it looks like：Put one bombing weekend on the same sheet as the three invisible months after it. The weekend is bright, bright enough that you want it to offset the months. No offset. Accountants do not allow that write-off. If you allow it, you are not his partner. You are his cosmetic accounting.

What it means：Backup is not an insult to your charm. Backup is a shift plan. You work Monday and Thursday. Someone else works the weekend visibility shift. ### Occupy first, define never

### The spend test

What it means：A person can screen for character, for how you treat each other, for values. Screening by transfer before you have met is not screening. It is harvest. Harvest dresses up as a test of whether you are generous. Generosity is an outcome, not a ticket. If the ticket is a screenshot of a transfer, you did not enter a romance. You entered a line.

What it looks like：Public talk keeps the same sequence. Contacts are exchanged. Nobody has sat down to eat. Reimbursement, a gift, or a pay-for-me is already on the table. What it means：A page with no flaws is not good character. It is good operations. People get sleepy, post blurry photos, and have dull Tuesdays. An account too meticulous to look like a life has its life somewhere else. If you are dating the account, the account will not spend Tuesday with you.

### No title, no transfer

What it means：Before the relationship has a definition, the transfer amount stays at zero. Zero is not stinginess. Zero is you refusing to pay wages during a trial. Gifts can happen after you both confirm what this is, and they remember a person. They do not unlock the next piece of content.

What it looks like：The least talented sentence in the experience threads is enough: if transfers and entry fees repeat before a relationship exists, stop. When you stop, a person with real interest gets annoyed once, then reschedules for a coffee. A harvest either vanishes or invents a new fee name. Once the name of the fee changes, you do not need a further debate.

### Say the budget once

What it means：Say the budget as one complete sentence, once. Do not explain your life. Do not attach a future rebate. If she starts negotiating the price of the rebate instead of how to spend this week, you are not in a relationship. You are in a contract talk, and she drafted the contract.

What it looks like：Composite: you say this month’s meals stay at an ordinary place. She says fine, then for two weeks she appears only when you offer an upgrade. Appearance follows the upgrade. It does not follow you. Remove the upgrade, and the person is removed. That removal is data, not your self-doubt.

### Seven days of verbs

What it means：Give yourself seven days. Log verbs, not adjectives. Adjectives are gentle, special, she gets you. Verbs are shows up, reschedules, introduces you, shares the load, refuses the roster. If the verbs miss the adjectives, delete the adjectives. Clear the head, and the exit has room.

What it looks like：People lose the review by replaying the tone of a voice note and not the schedule. The schedule is cold: three read-and-no-reply, zero times she asked about you, one request. You do not need a lecturer to shout that score. Read it. Then drop the star, and leave the field.
